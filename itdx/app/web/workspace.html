Mathematics and limitations

r = σ √(−2 ln(1−p)), with σ = 35 m and p = 0.95. This assumes independent, zero-mean Gaussian XY errors.

Measured coverage = errors inside the stated radius ÷ valid received reports. Bias can break the model. A stale observation is not a current position estimate.

Coverage is not a probability of truth, threat, intent or deception. No route feasibility or movement recommendation is computed.
