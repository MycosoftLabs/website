Form Space: mathematics first

The supplied Form Space source and NLM change history describe observations, latent representations, typed chart-local Form States and a posterior over organizing targets or attractors. Platonic-space principles motivate the architecture; demonstrated engineering objects must remain explicitly defined and tested.

Signals → calibrated features → latent state → chart-local Form State
Evidence + prior references → uncertainty, admissibility and model context
Task products → evaluator rubric → optional Borda ranking → human review
MINDEX preserves records; CREP/Earth Simulator displays spatial projections.

A complete Form State needs chart ID/version, coordinate definitions and units, an appropriate local metric, time/embodiment, evidence and prior references, uncertainty, transition rules and admissibility. Missing dimensions stay unknown. Language can express sourced reports and explain results; it is not the definition of the mathematical state.

Mathematical validation still required

The one-pager describes approximate embedding agreement as an equivalence relation. A distance threshold is not generally transitive: 0 is close to 0.75, and 0.75 to 1.5, while 0 and 1.5 may not be close. A quotient-space construction requires an actual equivalence relation or explicitly defined class membership. Chart-specific metrics, transitions, invariants and uncertainty calibration require separate tests.

The local app uses numerical features, chart IDs and provenance. This does not establish a fully learned atlas, validated transition maps, or the complete proposed NLM backbone.

Remaining exercise requirements

  1. Official scenario/inject files, expected outputs and exact definitions of the 1–5 values. Supplied task sheets mention five-point SME ratings for tasks 8 and 14; this does not define every exercise variable.
  2. Authorized reporting datasets and contracts for each source discipline, with timestamps, references, source lineage and any accepted truth labels.
  3. Versioned adapters that execute the intended NLM/MYCA/MINDEX services and record the actual backend. Health checks alone are insufficient.
  4. Trained checkpoints, chart/metric definitions, and independent assessment of accuracy, source claims and uncertainty.
  5. Freshness/coverage checks, a usable no-WebGL fallback for the public globe, and rehearsal on the demonstration machine.

Existing tests for duplicate reporting, aliases, conflicting sensors, embedded instructions, forged authenticators and stale versions support evidence-integrity evaluation. They do not constitute a complete counterintelligence or COIN exercise suite.

Borda and 1–5 values

First establish whether the numbers are ratings, ranks, confidence or scenario variables. Preserve their ordinal meaning. They are not automatically continuous Form Space coordinates or probabilities.

For m outputs, points = m − rank. Five distinct ranks receive 4, 3, 2, 1, 0. Ties share average occupied points. Partial ballots are rejected; whole-ballot abstention is allowed. In rating mode, higher ratings rank ahead and rating gaps are discarded. Freeze the output set and criterion; changing either can change the result.

Borda measures relative preference. Evidence validity and admissibility are evaluated separately. Fox and Bruyns discuss Borda scoring and incomplete-ballot variants.
